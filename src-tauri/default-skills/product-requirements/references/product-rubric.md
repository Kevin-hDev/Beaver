# Product Requirements Rubric

- A product problem names an affected user, current limitation, and consequence.
- A goal states an observable outcome, not a feature component.
- A requirement states needed behavior or policy, not architecture, files, APIs, schemas, or frameworks.
- A user story names one affected user, needed capability, and product outcome without estimating delivery work.
- A supplied story remains traceable by identifier or source anchor to the PRD decision it supports, while its backlog fields remain unchanged.
- An acceptance criterion states an observable pass or fail for one requirement or user story, including supported failure or boundary behavior when relevant.
- A non-goal excludes a plausible adjacent outcome and prevents scope drift.
- A success measure uses a known baseline and target or explicitly leaves measurement design open.
- A dependency is external to the requirement itself and must exist or be decided first.
- An assumption is plausible but unverified and must remain labeled.
- An open question names the decision, why it matters, and whether it blocks later work.
- A batch boundary never changes ordering, traceability, or coverage, and the continuation ledger accounts for every accepted item.
