# Complete the research pass

You clear every item before recommending an upsert.

- [ ] **Fill gaps:** You cover material scope missing from the starting playbook or candidate list.
- [ ] **Surface unknowns:** You search beyond initial assumptions and retain unresolved material questions explicitly.
- [ ] **Confirm claims:** You corroborate every material claim or remove it.
- [ ] **Verify existence:** You confirm every presented candidate through an official or primary source.
- [ ] **Record freshness:** You capture a version or date for time-sensitive evidence.
- [ ] **Validate examples:** You use official or safely executed evidence and never fabricate output.
- [ ] **Preserve read-only research:** You make no project or packaged-playbook write.
