01 /

All your models

Ollama offered and managed by Beaver, eleven cloud providers, and your OpenAI, Grok and Kimi web accounts. Local or remote, it builds.

02 /

It does the work

Files, web, Office documents. Subagents, each isolated in its own Git worktree. A Plan mode. A memory that holds.

03 /

While you sleep

Scheduled wakeups — one-time, daily, weekly — and Telegram, Slack, Discord to reach it from anywhere. You get up, it's done.

04 /

Your code, your Git

Branches, worktrees, commits, merges, guided conflicts. And a real terminal built in, on all three systems.

05 /

The web, right here

An embedded Chromium that spots the servers you have running and shows your pages without leaving Beaver. And it stays a real browser when you want to use it as one. On macOS and Windows.

06 /

And you can take it all back

Its tools, its system prompt, its interface: you replace whatever you want. That's the harness →

07 /

Your secrets stay secret

XChaCha20-Poly1305 vault, master key handed to your system's own secret store — macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, Linux Secret Service. The interface never sees a key. Never.
