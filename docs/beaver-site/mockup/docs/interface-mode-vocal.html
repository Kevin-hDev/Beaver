Speak instead of typing

Voice mode exists on macOS and Windows only. On Linux it is not shown at all: no microphone button in the composer, no section in the settings — the feature is absent rather than displayed broken. Everything on this page therefore concerns macOS and Windows.

A microphone button sits in the composer's action row. You press it, you speak, the text lands in the draft. The transcription runs on your machine, using a model you install once.

Nothing leaves the machine

The audio is captured, converted and transcribed locally. No recording is sent to any server, and nothing is sent to your model provider either: what reaches the agent is the text you have validated, exactly as if you had typed it.

The first time you open voice mode, a short dialog says it in the application's own words: “Audio is processed locally on this device and is never sent automatically.” You accept it once.

The only thing that travels over the network is the one-off download of the transcription model, from the public repository where it is published. After that, dictation works with no internet connection.

Note The audio itself is never written to a permanent file. Only the resulting text is kept, and only long enough to be inserted into your draft — or to be offered for recovery, as described below.

The three models

Beaver ships a fixed catalog of three transcription models. You pick one, it is downloaded, and it becomes the engine for your dictations. You can install several and switch between them.

The column that matters most is the third one, and it is not a preference — it is a property of the engine.

  • Parakeet TDT v3 and Qwen3-ASR 0.6B detect the spoken language by themselves. The language setting is then fixed on “Automatic detection” and cannot be changed: forcing a language on an engine that cannot consume one would be a lie in the interface.
  • Cohere Transcribe works the other way round: it needs to be told which language it is listening to. When you select it, the language setting becomes a real choice, with “Follow interface” as its starting point.

Qwen3-ASR 0.6B is also the only one to declare 22 regional variants on top of its thirty languages — Chinese provincial accents, Cantonese from Hong Kong and Guangdong, Wu, Min Nan.

Alongside your chosen model, Beaver installs a fourth, much smaller one: a 644 KB voice-activity detector. It is what tells silence from speech, so the dictation can stop by itself when you stop talking. It is not a transcription model and it is not something you choose.

Downloading a model, and resuming it

These are large files, and a large file gets interrupted. The download is built for that.

  • Progress is written to a checkpoint on disk — every 4 MB, and at most every 5 seconds. What the checkpoint records is only what is genuinely durable on the disk, not what has merely been received.
  • After an interruption, the transfer restarts from that point, not from zero. A closed application, a lost connection or a machine going to sleep costs you the last few megabytes, not the last gigabyte.
  • The server's file fingerprint is stored with the checkpoint. If the published file has changed in the meantime, the partial download is thrown away and started again instead of being stitched onto a different file.
  • The archive and each of its files carry a pinned SHA-256. A file that does not match is not installed.
  • Before starting, Beaver checks the free space for the archive, the installed model and a 64 MB margin. If it is not there, the download is refused with a clear message rather than filling your disk.
Where it lands Installed models go into voice-models/ inside the data folder, and a download in progress into voice-models/partials/. Removing a model frees the whole thing.

The settings

Settings › Advanced › Voice mode. The section gives the on/off switch and the model; the Advanced button opens the full panel, with the installed models, the microphone and the memory setting.

Two of them deserve a word.

  • Microphone gain amplifies the signal before transcription, for a voice the machine hears too faintly. It starts at +6 dB rather than 0: a laptop microphone at arm's length is usually below what the model wants.
  • Unload model decides how long the model stays in memory after a dictation. Keeping it loaded makes the next dictation start instantly; unloading it immediately gives the memory back. Two minutes is the middle ground.

The panel also has a Try button, which runs a dictation that goes nowhere: the text is displayed for you to judge, along with the compute time and the delay before the text appears. It is the honest way to compare two models on your own voice and your own microphone, since the panel otherwise only shows the last measured speed, as a multiple of real time.

The keyboard

Two different things, and they do not work the same way.

The keys, and when they apply
KeyWhat it doesWhen
Your shortcutStarts a dictation; during one, ends it and transcribesAnywhere in the application, once you have set one
EnterEnds the dictation and transcribesWhile listening only
EscapeCancels the dictation in progressAt any stage other than idle

There is no shortcut by default: you create one yourself, in the voice panel, by pressing the combination you want. Beaver refuses it if it collides with one of its own shortcuts, or with the seven system combinations it will not take over: ⌘/Ctrl plus A, C, V, X, Z, Q, W.

Enter and Escape are ignored when Shift is held, while a Chinese, Japanese or Korean input method is composing, and when the cursor is in a dialog or in the integrated terminal — so they keep their usual meaning wherever they already had one.

Recovering an interrupted dictation

You have spoken for two minutes and something goes wrong: you hit Escape by mistake, or you close the conversation the text was going into. The words are not lost.

  • Past 30 seconds of actual speech, a cancelled dictation is not thrown away: its text is put aside, and a line appears under the composer offering to restore it, with the length of what was captured.
  • Below that threshold it is discarded straight away. A three-second false start is not worth a recovery prompt.
  • The offer stands for 10 minutes, then it expires on its own. You can also dismiss it yourself.
  • The same thing happens when the text was ready but its destination had gone: closing the draft during transcription turns the result into a recovery instead of losing it.
