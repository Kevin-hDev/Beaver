Importing from another agent

If you already use Claude Code, Codex or another agent, you do not have to rewrite everything. Beaver detects nine applications installed on your machine and brings over their instructions, their rules and their skills.

What the import does

The import brings over three kinds of content: the instruction document, that is, the file the assistant reads at every conversation; the rules, instruction files filed in a rules/ folder; and the skills, procedures filed in a skills/ folder.

What is not imported The import brings over your instructions, your rules and your skills. It does not bring over your conversations, your API keys, your connectors or your interface settings.

Two particularities are worth pointing out. Hermes Agent has no instruction document taken over — only its rules and its skills. And OpenClaw is the most complex case: several workspaces are examined, and the document kept is the first AGENTS.md found.

Two sources depend on environment variables, which explains most non-detections: OpenCode is looked for in $XDG_CONFIG_HOME/opencode, or ~/.config/opencode if the variable is not set; Kimi Code in $KIMI_CODE_HOME, or ~/.kimi-code by default, plus three additional locations.

Assistant not detected Detection relies on the presence of a folder at a specific location. If your assistant is installed elsewhere — in particular through an environment variable for OpenCode and Kimi Code — it will not be found.

What is taken, and where it lands

The instruction document is copied into Beaver's AGENTS.md, with a fingerprint of the source to detect later modifications. The rules and the skills are copied into the matching folders in the data folder. Each imported document can then be enabled or disabled individually, without being deleted.

How it goes

  • The import assistant detects the applications present by looking for their folders.
  • You choose the sources to take from, and what you want from each one.
  • Beaver copies the selected content.
  • A backup of the previous state is kept.

The import is offered during the onboarding flow, and stays available afterwards.

The backups

Five backups at most are kept per document. They live in agent-import-backups/, inside the data folder, and the record of what has been imported is in external-agent-sources.json.

Nothing is overwritten without a backup Beaver keeps up to five earlier versions of each imported document. Your previous instruction file is not lost.
