Finding your way among the models

Configure two or three providers and several hundred models become available — one of them is a reseller that offers hundreds on its own. This page explains where that list comes from, what Beaver knows about each model, and how to keep the ones you really use within reach.

Where the model list comes from

Beaver combines three sources, which explains why some models are better documented than others:

  • What the provider announces. Every provider exposes the list of models your account can reach. That is the source of truth on availability.
  • Beaver's internal registry, which fills in the missing information for known models.
  • A public catalog, downloaded and kept up to date, which supplies capabilities and pricing.

The consequence: a very recent model can show up with incomplete information, until the catalogs list it. It remains usable.

The ability to use tools is the first criterion in Beaver. A model without it holds a conversation but remains incapable of the slightest action. That is the main cause of disappointment when a model is chosen on its reputation.

Warning Check first that the model can use tools. That is what separates an assistant able to act from a model that merely replies. An excellent model without that capability will be of almost no use to you in Beaver.

Favorites

A model can be pinned. Favorites are stored locally, in a file of their own, and identify each model by its provider and its name — so the same model at two providers counts as two separate entries.

The write is atomic: temporary file, then rename. An interruption at the wrong moment cannot leave a corrupted list of favorites.

Adding a favorite that is already there, or removing one that is not, raises no error: the operation simply has no effect, and the file is not even rewritten.

Note Favorites are local. They live in an application file, not in an account: moving to another machine means setting them up again.

Choosing a model for a conversation

The selector sits in the input bar, under the message field — not at the top of the conversation. See Interface overview.

The choice applies to the current conversation. Switching model mid-way is possible: the rest of the conversation uses the new one.

Note The same model at two providers is two entries. Prices, limits and availability can differ from one provider to another for an identical model.
