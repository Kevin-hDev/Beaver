One conversation, one environment

Every conversation carries its own settings: its model, its permissions, its working directory, its tools. Moving from one to another changes your working context, not just the discussion thread. This page explains how you navigate between them and how you organize them.

What belongs to each conversation

A conversation has its own settings, not the application's:

  • its model and its provider;
  • its permission mode;
  • its Fast mode, controlled from the input bar: it asks the provider for priority processing where the provider offers it, and has no effect with the others;
  • its working directory;
  • its active tools;
  • its enabled connectors;
  • its Git branch;
  • its terminal and browser tabs.

Moving between conversations

  • The sidebar lists the conversations. It is the only way to move from one to another: conversations do not open as tabs.
  • New conversation: ⌥⌘N (Alt+Ctrl+N).
  • The sidebar hides with ⌘B (Ctrl+B).
  • The order of the list is yours, not a sort by date: you decide where each conversation sits, and that is kept from one launch to the next.
  • A conversation can be pinned to stay at the top of the list, with an order of its own among pinned conversations.

Clone tabs

The only tabs a conversation has are those created by cloning.

  • A conversation and its clones form a group of three tabs at most.
  • A tab can be renamed and closed.
  • Closing a tab can also clean up the Git branch attached to it.

The details are in Cloning a conversation.

Archiving, restoring, renaming, deleting

  • A finished conversation is archived rather than deleted: it leaves the main list without being lost.
  • Archived conversations are viewed in Settings › Archived chats, and restored from there.
  • Permanent deletion exists too: archiving is not the only way out.
  • A conversation can be renamed, unless it is a child session.
  • Subagents follow a separate archiving path, and refuse archiving in certain states.
  • Cloning an archived conversation produces a clone that is not archived.
Note Archive rather than delete: an archived conversation leaves the main list without being erased. You find it again in Settings › Archived chats.

Search

The ⌘G (Ctrl+G) shortcut opens a search box that lets you find a conversation and go to it.

Warning Search covers the conversation name and the project name, never the content of the messages. A sentence said in the middle of an exchange cannot be found this way. Subagents and clones are excluded from the results.

Exporting a conversation to Markdown

A conversation can be exported to Markdown. That is the way to get an exchange out of the application: personal archiving, sharing, offline reading.

What a conversation keeps

  • 2,000 messages at most per conversation. Beyond that, sending is refused — nothing is truncated or compressed automatically.
  • One conversation per file, in agent-sessions/.
  • Conversations created automatically by a wakeup carry a marker that sets them apart.
  • Large tool results are stored separately, in tool-results/, so as not to bloat the conversation file.
  • Every conversation keeps a record of its stream failures and its diagnostics; those histories are not carried over into a clone.
