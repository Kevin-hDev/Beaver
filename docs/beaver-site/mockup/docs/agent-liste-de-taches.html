The task list

On a long stretch of work, the agent breaks the task into steps itself and keeps a list visible live: what is done, what is in progress, what is left. Two benefits — you follow progress without rereading the whole conversation, and the agent keeps track of what it has to do instead of drifting.

A feature to enable The task list is an optional tool, disabled by default. If you have seen this feature elsewhere, you will not find it until you enable the matching group in Settings › Tools.

What a task contains

Each task carries a title, a wording in the progressive form used while it runs, and a status out of three: to do, in progress, done.

Task series

Tasks are grouped into series. A series corresponds to one piece of work, with its own title.

The three states of a series
StateMeaning
ActiveBeing processed
PausedSuspended, with a recorded reason
DoneAll the tasks are complete

A series moves automatically to “done” when all its tasks are — and an empty series is never considered done.

The five tools

What the feature is made of
ToolRole
todo_writeThe agent creates or updates its tasks
todo_historyView past series
todo_pauseSuspend the current series, with a reason
todo_resumeResume a suspended series
todo_deleteDelete a series

Four of them — all but todo_write — stay available in Plan mode, which lets you view and steer the lists during an exploration phase.

When the agent neglects its list

When a series is active and the agent makes no progress on it across exchanges, Beaver steps in: after two turns without progress, it is prompted; after four turns, the series is paused automatically.

Why A list abandoned halfway must not stay marked as active indefinitely: the progress shown would become false.

The limits

Reference values

Beyond twenty series, the oldest ones are dropped.
