02 / The harness

Built your way.Really.

Not a fork. Your Beaver. An extension doesn't only add: it takes away and it replaces. The same Beaver, rewired by you.

my-extension.ts — beaverApi: "1"
export default defineExtension({
  activate(beaver) {
    // your tool, next to Beaver's own
    beaver.registerTool(myTool)

    // your view, inside its interface
    beaver.ui.register({ type: 'tab', id: 'crm' })

    // and what you override — advanced API
    beaver.unstable.registerReplacement(myBash)
  },
})
01

Replace its tools

Its bash tool doesn't suit you? Override it with your own, through the advanced API. The agent will only see yours.

02

Rewrite its brain

The system prompt can be replaced, switched off, or reverted to the model's own — from the settings, separately for agent mode and chat mode.

03

Add your panels and your views

The interface isn't fixed: your panels, your buttons and your views take their place next to its own.

04

Without ever breaking everything

Each extension runs in its own process, outside Beaver's core. One crashes, Beaver carries on. And nothing activates without your explicit consent.
